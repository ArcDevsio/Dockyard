## About

Dockyard is the community content distribution repository for **Dockyard**, an independent platform for discovering and managing content for **Spaceflight Simulator**.

This repository is used to organize information and distribution resources for supported community-created content.

Supported content may include:

* Mods
* Parts
* Textures
* Blueprints

## Content Submission

Creators may request to have their work included in Dockyard.

Each submission should provide the necessary information for proper attribution and compatibility.

This may include:

* Content files
* Content name
* Creator name
* Version
* Supported game version
* Description
* Dependencies
* License or redistribution permission
* Preview images, where applicable

Content is reviewed before being made available through Dockyard.

---

## Creator Ownership

Creators retain ownership of their original work.

Submitting content for inclusion in Dockyard does not transfer ownership to ArcDevsio.

Dockyard distributes submitted content only with appropriate permission from the creator or rights holder.

---

## Content Categories

### Mods

Gameplay modifications, utilities, systems, and other supported modifications.

### Parts

Custom spacecraft parts, engines, structural components, and related assets.

### Textures

Texture packs and visual modifications.

### Blueprints

Community-created spacecraft, rockets, vehicles, and other designs.
