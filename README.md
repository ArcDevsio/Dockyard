## About

Dockyard is a community content distribution platform for **Spaceflight Simulator**.

It provides a centralized place for compatible community-created content, allowing users to discover and download supported mods, parts, textures, blueprints, and other content.

This repository is primarily used for Dockyard's content distribution infrastructure.

## For Creators

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Creator-Ownership%20Retained-0d0d0d?style=for-the-badge" alt="Creator Ownership">

</div>

Creators may request to have their work included in Dockyard.

A submission may include:

* Content files
* Content name
* Creator name
* Version
* Supported game version
* Description
* Required dependencies
* License or redistribution permission
* Preview images, where applicable

<br>

### Creator Ownership

Creators retain ownership of their original work.

Submitting content to Dockyard does not transfer ownership to ArcDevsio.

Dockyard only distributes community-created content with appropriate permission from the creator or rights holder.

## Content Review

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Review-Required-0d0d0d?style=for-the-badge" alt="Review Required">

</div>

Content may be reviewed before being included in Dockyard.

Content may be rejected or removed if it:

* Contains malicious or harmful files
* Misrepresents another creator's work
* Violates licensing or redistribution permissions
* Presents a security concern
* Is incompatible with supported systems
* Violates applicable laws
* Violates Dockyard content guidelines

## Distribution

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Distribution-Centralized-0d0d0d?style=for-the-badge" alt="Centralized Distribution">
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Content-Community%20Created-0d0d0d?style=for-the-badge" alt="Community Created">

</div>

Dockyard provides a centralized way for users to discover and access supported community content.

The Dockyard application can use content metadata to identify available files, versions, compatibility information, and updates.

```text
Content
│
├── Metadata
│   ├── Name
│   ├── Author
│   ├── Version
│   └── Compatibility
│
└── Files
    ├── Content
    └── Assets
```

## Licensing

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Licensing-Per%20Content-0d0d0d?style=for-the-badge" alt="Per Content Licensing">

</div>

Individual content may have its own license or redistribution terms.

A license that applies to the Dockyard repository does **not automatically apply** to third-party mods, textures, parts, blueprints, or other community-created content.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, community-created content remains the property of its respective creator.
